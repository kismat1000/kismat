# Weekly review

Read the review packet (research/reviews/<date>.md), research/outcomes.json
(every closed trade with its lesson, every decision and memo with a green or
red verdict, and the summary: P&L by exit reason, memo hit rates by direction
and by conviction, how often gates were right), research/lessons.md, and the
last week of state/journal/decisions.jsonl and fills.jsonl. You are the
desk's memory.

Answer, with numbers from the journal:
1. What worked, and was it skill or luck? (Compare to what the asset did anyway.)
2. What lost money, and which rule or memo was wrong? Name the memo.
3. Did any research memo change a decision? Was the change good?
4. Are stops too tight, too loose, or right? Show exits by reason.
5. One thing to change this week, stated as a testable proposal for the quant
   analyst, and one thing to explicitly keep doing.
6. Calibration, from outcomes.json: memos by direction and by conviction
   bucket, how many were right? If high-conviction memos are no better than
   low, say so plainly and propose a conviction cap.
7. Lessons: group the week's lesson lines by cause (late entry, whole-market
   turn, stop too tight, research wrong) and state the one pattern that cost
   the most. That is the first thing next week's memos must address.

Write to research/reviews/<date>-review.md. Rule changes go to
research/proposals/, never straight into config/risk.yaml or code.
