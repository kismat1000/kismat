This is where the week's real finding is, and it reverses what I have been reporting. The mechanism that matters: a flat memo scores exactly 0.0 — conviction is discarded — so it applies a 40% haircut rather than expressing "no view". Writing *no memo* is the neutral act.

A correction to my own claim from yesterday. I wrote that "every memo is a drag, including the longs", and lumped the two cases together. That was over-stated. The defect is narrower than I said: it is flat → 0.0 specifically. The fix in research/proposals/2026-09-19-conviction-means-two-things.md §1 stands; recommendation §2 (cut RESEARCH_WEIGHT) is weaker than I made it sound and should not be actioned on yesterday's reasoning alone.
